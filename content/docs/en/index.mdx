lifegui is a self-hosted personal organizer. It brings tasks, habits, an Obsidian-compatible second brain and your Google Calendar into one place. You can also talk to it from Claude or ChatGPT through MCP.

![lifegui dashboards](/screenshots/dashboards.png)

<Callout type="info">
  The app interface is in Brazilian Portuguese. These docs keep the interface labels in their original form, in bold, with the English meaning in parentheses on first use. For example: **Configurações** (Settings).
</Callout>

## Features

<Cards>
  <Card title="Tasks" href="/docs/features/tasks" description="Kanban per category with due dates, priorities, subtasks and drag and drop." />
  <Card title="Habits" href="/docs/features/habits" description="Weekly targets, daily check-ins, streaks and consistency heatmaps." />
  <Card title="Brain" href="/docs/features/brain" description="Markdown notes in an Obsidian-compatible vault, with tags, wikilinks, backlinks and a graph view." />
  <Card title="Mobile capture" href="/docs/features/mobile-capture" description="Send ideas to the Brain inbox from your phone." />
  <Card title="Calendar" href="/docs/features/calendar" description="Google Calendar as the source of truth. Schedule tasks, habits and notes." />
  <Card title="Dashboards" href="/docs/features/dashboards" description="One dashboard per area, with heatmaps, an adherence radar and the weekly flow." />
  <Card title="MCP server" href="/docs/features/mcp" description="Let Claude and ChatGPT read your day and create tasks, captures and events." />
  <Card title="Settings" href="/docs/features/settings" description="Turn modules on and off, connect integrations and manage tokens." />
</Cards>

lifegui is multi-user. Each user has isolated data and their own vault, and each user turns modules on and off.
